## Improvements

- Monitor: the image reaches the Mac about 37 ms sooner. bmonitor now shows each video packet as soon as it arrives, instead of holding the 60 ms buffer the iPhone asks for. Measured glass to glass on Wi-Fi, without extra stutter or lost packets.
- Webcam: on top of that, each frame reaches calls up to 20 ms sooner. The camera extension picks up a frame the moment bmonitor sends it, and turning and scaling the frame now runs in one GPU pass.
