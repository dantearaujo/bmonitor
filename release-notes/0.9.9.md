## Fixes

- After updating to 0.9.8, the window could open small, with the video standing up and the script open: the control bar and the status didn't fit and slid under the script. The video now never gets narrower than the control bar needs, and the window can't be resized below that.

- When the app opened with the video standing up and the script open, the picture and the controls slid to the right for about a second before settling. The status capsule no longer pushes them while the app waits for the first frame, and bmonitor reopens with the last camera's format.
