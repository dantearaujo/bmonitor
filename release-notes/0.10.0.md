## New

- **Webcam:** turn on Camera › Use as Webcam and your iPhone becomes a camera called "bmonitor" in Meet, Zoom, FaceTime and any other app. It keeps Blackmagic Camera's manual exposure, white balance, focus and LUT, which Continuity Camera doesn't give you. Video only: keep using the microphone you already use.
- The first time, macOS asks you to allow the camera extension. bmonitor opens a short guide and takes System Settings straight to Camera Extensions.
- While a call is using the camera, the status capsule shows "Webcam". With bmonitor closed or the iPhone disconnected, the call sees the bmonitor icon with a pause sign instead of a frozen picture.

## Em português

Só vídeo: o som continua no microfone que você já usa.
