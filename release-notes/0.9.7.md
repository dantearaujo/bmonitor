## Script

- A script panel inside the monitor window. Paste or open your text (Markdown works) and read it while you record. ⌘2 opens and closes it beside the video or, with the video in landscape, above or below it.
- Follow speech: the script moves on its own as you talk, using the camera's sound. Recognition runs on the Mac, offline.
- Pop the script out into its own window and move it anywhere; one click docks it back.
- ↑ and ↓ move between sentences; click a paragraph to jump to it.

## App

- New app icon: the iPhone's rear camera inside a viewfinder, with the recording light on.
- Frames per second has its own item in the control bar, right after Shutter (key P).
- New About window, with links to the project and to support it.
- Updates now show up on their own when you open the app, with what changed.
